Three Fast Weeks and a First Taste of Paid Programming

Well, so many things have happened since the last time I was here. Actually the pace of change was so fast and I was so focused on each my projects, that I couldn't find some time to come here and write about them all. So, here I am after almost 3 weeks, writing about what happened.

Naar

One day, around May 27, one interesting idea came to my mind: what if there was a package manager for AI skills? Just for some context, skills are markdown files you add to your projects that gives rules and instructions to you AI coding assistant of choice to help it do something specific better. Have you watched the famed movie The Matrix from 1999? In that movie, poeple can get hooke up to computers and there are some diskets that upon insertion to the computer, teaches them something specific. For example, they could learn flying with a helicopter, or karate, all in a few hours of consuming the contents of a specific respective disket. Skills are the same thing but for AI agents.

So, after a little bit of research, I found out that there's nothing out there for safely installing skills. The idea was to create a CLI dev tool that helps developers find and install their needed skills right from their working terminal. It took me almost a day to get a full v0.1.0 prototype and within around 8 days, I shipped the v1.0.0! It was a fantastic journey. I still don't know how I can find new users, but for now its sitting on my Github with an MIT license and ready for new users to test and use and give me some feedback. I appreciate your feedback on Naar if you ever go there and test it.

DDero

One weekend evening I went to see my best friend, MH. He told me that he had an idea to make a business in which he helps small to medium businesses grow their online presence. He called it DDero. He'd had this idea since 10 years ago, but this time he was serious about turning it into something real. So, he told me about it, and I told him about recent projects I hand worked on, and suddenly he proposed we could work on a project together. I accepted. A few days later he called and said there was a prestigious businessman who needs his website to be rebuilt from scratch. I had some ideas. He assembled a team in a few hours, just like in movies. We had an online session and then we discussed what could be done.

That night I couldn't sleep. I spent that entire night until early morning making a prototype of what could be done based on the information I had of the client. I showed it to MH in the afternoon and he was thrilled. Then he went to see the client and showed the first prototype and apparently the client was shocked to see how fast we could patch up a working prototype. Not only he accepted our offer of working with him and making his website, but also he brought us another customer!

Right now I'm so busy with all of these projects that I'm about to lose my sanity. I relaxed a little bit today. It was a boring day. But I worked on BluLexi (I promise I'll talk about it soon) a little bit, and that was all. I'm so thrilled to see how all of this come together by the end of summer. This is the first time I'm earning money directly with programming.
